Telemetry audit section C item 6.  Project `hoghunter` already exists in
org `simple-with-us`.  Nothing in this change runs on Jay's Mac.

## What landed

- SPM `sentry-cocoa` (`minorVersion: 9.30.1`, up to next minor) on
  `HogHunter` and `HogHunterIOS`.  The widget target is not linked.
  9.x's Swift 6.2 manifest depends on KSCrash, so `from` (up-to-next-major)
  cannot resolve; XcodeGen `minorVersion` is the pin that SPM accepts.
- `Sources/Telemetry/SentryTelemetry.swift`: DSN from Info.plist only,
  `sendDefaultPii = false`, traces 0.2, continuous profiling 0.1 with
  trace lifecycle, app hangs at 2s, 5xx capture, URL redaction.  Session
  Replay is off.  Empty / unexpanded `$(SENTRY_DSN)` is a no-op.
- `macos/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` and `ios/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`.
- App Store answers: `ios/AppStore/privacy-answers.md`.
- TestFlight archive passes `SENTRY_DSN` into `xcodebuild` and uploads
  dSYMs with `sentry-cli` when `SENTRY_AUTH_TOKEN` is set.  A missing
  token does not fail the ship.
- `scripts/install.sh` forwards `SENTRY_DSN_MACOS` or `SENTRY_DSN` the
  same way, and never prints the value.
- `.github/workflows/sentry-ci-report.yml` reports CI failures to
  fleet-infra.  Fingerprint `[ci-failure, hoghunter, workflow]`.
